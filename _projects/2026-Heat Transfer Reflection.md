This problem provided an opportunity to apply the heat conduction equation to a composite wall system with internal heat generation. The surface energy balance was needed for finding specific regions in the wall, which allowed me to find unknown values like the volumetric heat generation rate and material conductivity. The assignment also served as a good introduction to how temperature sensors can be used in systems with many layers, which is a common scenario in real world engineering systems. Understanding how to analyze heat transfer through composite walls with generation and convection boundaries is useful for thermal systems, which I can use in the future of my engineering career.
